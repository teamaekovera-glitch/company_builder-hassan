# AURELITH — SERIES BIBLE (CANON)

This document is the locked canon for the Aurelith Encyclopedia (60 entries, 6 audits). Hierarchy of authority: **this bible > master timeline > glossary > individual entries.** Entries may extend the canon sideways (new towns, people, customs, minor events) but may never contradict it. Every new proper noun and dated event invented in an entry must be logged in that entry's "New Canon" and "New Timeline" blocks for harvest into the master files.

## 1. COSMOLOGY AND THE VEILFALL

Aurelith is a single great continent bounded by the **Marrow Sea** (west and south) and the **Hushed Ocean** (east and north), with scattered archipelagos — the largest being the **Drathmark Isles**. Two moons cross the night sky: the slow white **Liral**, and **Corm**, the swift copper second moon.

In **Year 0 AR**, Corm shattered. The night called the **Veilfall** broke the moon into a copper arc of debris that still rings the world. For a decade afterward shards rained down (the **Raining Years**), tides shifted, and threads of the moon's substance soaked into the crust, pooling as **veins** of loose light. The shattered moon's glow is called **the Veil**. Magic in Aurelith — **Veilwork** — is the craft of drawing heat, light, force, or memory-shape from the Veil through a vein, always at a price paid from the worker's own memories. Nothing is borrowed, only traded: this is the First Law of Veilwork.

**Calendar.** The world reckons time in the **Aurelith Reckoning (AR)**, counted from the Veilfall. Twelve months of thirty days (**Thaw, Sowing, Highwater, Milk, Hotmouth, Reaping, Goldleaf, Ember, Fogmonth, Firstfrost, Longnight, Wolfmoon**) plus five intercalary **Veil Days**, a festival of remembrance. The present year is **1147 AR**. Dates are always written "Year X AR" or "X AR."

**The Silent Age.** The era before Year 0 left no surviving writing; what is "known" of it is oral memory only, and historians call it the years without names. No entry may give Silent Age precise dates.

## 2. THE TEN ERAS (short titles locked; each gets a full entry)

1. **The Silent Age** — before Year 0. Oral memory only.
2. **The Driftwood Age** (0–180 AR) — resettlement, migration, the first Veil-workers.
3. **The Chartering** (180–412) — maritime law, the rise of the Merchant Tables, the naming of the continent.
4. **The Lantern Centuries** (412–655) — movable type, universities, the Umbral Scholasticate.
5. **The Long Quarrel** (655–831) — the wars of the Ledger and the Tide; ended by the Concord of Bells.
6. **The Ledger Peace** (831–980) — Liorne's banking hegemony; first futures markets.
7. **The Friction Wars** (980–1071) — veinstone engines militarize industry and war.
8. **The Concordance** (1071–1120) — postwar treaties, the heliograph web, the first aerostats.
9. **The Emberspring** (1120–1140) — the shard-battery boom, labor uprisings, the Ashling famine.
10. **The Present Height** (1140–1147) — the Shard Rush, the Rate War, the Tsek succession.

## 3. THE TWELVE NATIONS (names, capitals, founding dates LOCKED)

Adjectival rule: use the nation name as the adjective ("Vessany pine," "Maravand guilds") except where a fixed form is listed.

1. **Caldrith** — Maritime kingdom of the western capes. Capital **Solvenna**. Constitutional crown limited by the **Merchant Tables** (chartered 203 AR; **Table Rights** constitution of 1012). Language Caldrin. Salt-fish, shipwrights, charters, stubborn law.
2. **The Zharkun Khanate** — Steppe empire turned settled state. Capital **Ulzai**. Unified in **88 AR** by **Khagan Burei** (Battle of the Red Tent); settled in the 340s. Governing assembly: the **Kurultai**. Language Zharkuni. Horse law, felt cities, long memory.
3. **The Nine Banners of Tsek** — Bureaucratic examination empire of the river plains of the east. Capital **Yandu**, founded 151 by **Emperor Diao Zhen**. Examinations instituted **402**, reformed by **Empress Diao Wen** (r. 512–560). Language Tseki. Paper, granaries, precedent.
4. **Vessany** — Boreal principalities of the Pinemarrow forests. Principal town **Zimovets**. **Stewards' Council** from 601; the **Iron Regency** (1011–1061) under **Yelena Vasko** (regent 1023–1061). Language Vessanic. Timber, iron, long winters, hard bargains.
5. **Maravand** — Federation of desert guild-cities of the Qassis basin. Chief cities **Qasr-Ilim** and **Surafta**. The **Water Code** of Mira of the Nine Wells adopted 771. Language Maravandi. Qanats, water-shares, guest-law.
6. **Drathmark** — Volcanic archipelago thalassocracy of the storm isles. Seat **Skarnholt**; leadership by **Kingsmoot**. Language Drathic (minor — no entry). Stormglass, sea-roads, oath-fires.
7. **Liorne** — Canal republic of the southern lagoons. The city of **Liorne** is the state. **Council of Ten** (government) — do not confuse with the Merchant Tables. Banks from the 840s; movable type from **438**. Language Liornan (minor). Glass, opera, compound interest.
8. **The Ashling Territories** — Highland clan confederation with no capital; assembly at **Caer Luth**. Language Ashling (minor). Oath-law, bards called **weighers of words**, the Oath-ledger, post-famine migration (famine 1128–1131).
9. **Sanavere** — Temple-state of the great river delta (River **Senn**). Capital **Sovanel**. Faith: the **Tide-That-Remembers**. Bellfoundries; heretic-saint **Ilba the Younger** (581–633).
10. **The Kettle Freeholds** — Confederation of miner commons under the **Kettledeep Ridges**. Seat **Hearth Delve**. First syndical charter **1044**. Language Kettlish (minor). Radical democracy, arc batteries, the Iron Sermon.
11. **Sarhadd** — Monsoon sultanate of the southern monsoon coasts. Capital **Suramala**. The **Pearl Fleet**, pepper, tea, shipwrights. Language Sarhaddi (minor).
12. **The Umbral Concord** — Polar scholasticate of the night lands. Seat **Vigil**; keeps the **Long Count** observatory. Language Umbral. The Blank Page; smallest population; keeper of the calendar.

## 4. THE TEN MAJOR FIGURES (names and dates LOCKED)

1. **Aurelith the Navigator** (198–241) — cartographer who surveyed the Marrow Sea (219–233); died at sea; the continent named for her in 260.
2. **Khagan Burei** (—118) — unifier of the steppe, founder of the Zharkun Khanate.
3. **Emperor Diao Zhen** (—178) — founder of Tsek, builder of Yandu.
4. **Empress Diao Wen** (463–560, r. 512–560) — examination reformer; opened the trials to commoner districts.
5. **Ilba the Younger** (581–633) — Sanaveri heretic-saint; preached that the Tide forgets on purpose.
6. **Mira of the Nine Wells** (689–762) — Maravandi water-law philosopher; author of the Water Code.
7. **Ragnvald Ashsmouth** (763–801) — Drathic storm-king who burned his own fleet at the **Maw of Grief** (796) to force the peace that became the Concord of Bells.
8. **Yelena Vasko** (994–1061) — Vessany's Iron Regent; industrializer and constitutionalist.
9. **Ottokar Brim, "Old Ottokar"** (b. 1081, living) — Kettle Freeholds syndicalist organizer; face of the trimmer-rights movement.
10. **Captain Peregrine Vale** (1077–1138) — Liorne-born aerostat pioneer; first flight **1109**.

## 5. THE EIGHT RELIGIONS AND PHILOSOPHIES

1. **The Tide-That-Remembers** (Sanavere) — ancestor-tide pantheism; the dead return on the tide; bells carry names against forgetting.
2. **The Ledger of Deeds** (Liorne, Caldrith) — every deed is an entry; confession is an audit; grace is a balanced book.
3. **The Blank Page** (Umbral Concord) — apophatic scholasticism; the sacred is precisely what cannot be written.
4. **The Stormsaints** (Drathmark) — the Storm-Father and nine mortal saints; oath-fires on headlands.
5. **The Quiet Assay** (Ashling Territories) — words have weight; speech is spending; silence is savings.
6. **The Cult of the Second Moon** (dispersed) — post-Veilfall shard worship; the Shattered is not the Lost.
7. **The Thousand Hearths** (Vessany/Tsek borderlands) — hearth-god ancestor practice, one flame per family line.
8. **The Iron Sermon** (Kettle Freeholds) — labor-faith of craft-sainthood and strike-prayer; every tool a relic.

## 6. THE EIGHT TECHNOLOGIES AND MAGIC SYSTEMS

1. **Veilwork** — the magic system. Drawing heat, light, force, or memory-shape from the Veil through veins; price paid in the worker's own memories. Overdrawing causes **the pale fade**, progressive memory-loss ending in identity death. Skilled practitioners: Veil-workers; the old guild term is **tide-sworn**.
2. **Qanat engineering** (Maravand) — the civic art of underground water channels; the engineering that made the Water Code possible.
3. **The BellFoundry method** (Sanavere) — acoustic engineering: bell-resonance used for signaling, medicine, and the great peace bells of 831.
4. **Movable-type printing** (Liorne, from 438) — the codex economy of the Lantern Centuries.
5. **Stormglass navigation** (Drathmark) — weather-glass instruments that "remember" storm-paths; the safety of the sea-roads.
6. **Double-entry ledgers and futures** (Liorne) — financial technology; grain futures from 874.
7. **The heliograph web and aerostats** (Concordance era) — signal towers spanning the continent (completed 1098); the first aerostat flight 1109.
8. **Veinstone arc batteries** (Emberspring, from 1121) — industrialized Veilwork: crystallized vein-matter arcs powering engines, tended by **trimmers**, workers paid in wages *and* compensating memories. The labor-abuse engine of the present era.

## 7. THE SIX LANGUAGES (each gets a full entry with grammar and vocabulary tables)

1. **Caldrin** (Caldrith) — analytic, chart-obsessed vocabulary, ritual numerals.
2. **Zharkuni** (Zharkun Khanate) — agglutinative, rich in saddle and road terms, evidentiality marking.
3. **Tseki** (Nine Banners) — isolating, particle grammar, register system (plain, formal, memorial).
4. **Vessanic** (Vessany) — fusional, case-rich, winter and kinship vocabulary.
5. **Maravandi** (Maravand) — root-and-pattern morphology, water-law terminology.
6. **Umbral** (Umbral Concord) — contemplative constructed language; evidential particles for darkness (heard / inferred / counted).

Minor languages (named, no entry): Drathic, Ashling, Liornan, Sanaveri, Sarhaddi, Kettlish.

## 8. THE SIX ECONOMIC SYSTEMS

1. **The Merchant Tables** (Caldrith) — chartered federated trade; the Table Rights.
2. **Water-share commons** (Maravand) — water shares as currency and law.
3. **Ledger banking and futures** (Liorne) — joint-stock banks, insurance, grain futures.
4. **The Oath-economy** (Ashling Territories) — debt of honor; gift-debt ledgers kept by weighers.
5. **The Syndical Halls** (Kettle Freeholds) — worker-owned mining charters; the hearth-vote.
6. **The Aerostat Cartel and shard futures** (present day) — the Rate War (1145–), shard-futures markets, trimmer labor.

## 9. THE PRESENT SITUATION (Year 1147 AR — locked state of the world)

- The **Shard Rush** (1143–): prospectors race to register new veins across the north; Vessany's Stewards' Council claims oversight; Caldrith Tables fund the expeditions.
- The **Rate War** (1145–): the Aerostat Cartel vs. the Freeholds' haulage halls; freight rates as a weapon.
- **Tsek succession**: Emperor **Diao Kezhi**, 71, childless; no designated heir; the examination elite fracture into memorial factions.
- **Post-famine Ashling**: migration into Vessany and Caldrith; oath-ledgers used to hold distant clans together.
- **Trimmer rights**: Old Ottokar's movement to cap memory-tithe in the battery halls; the Iron Sermon spreads along the freight roads.
- The calendar: it is autumn of 1147; Reaping has ended; the Long Count observatory at Vigil has just announced a **dark conjunction** of Liral and the Veil-arc for Firstfrost of this year (an omen every faith reads differently).

## 10. STYLE AND PROCESS RULES (binding on every entry)

- Entry body **≥ 2,300 words** (verified with `wc -w`, excluding New Canon/New Timeline blocks).
- Entry template, in order: `# Title` — one-line epigraph (invented in-world quotation with attribution and year AR) — `## Overview` — `## History` — `## Government and Society` (or the section set appropriate to the entry type) — `## Culture` — `## Foreign Relations` — `## The Present Height (Year 1147)` — `## In-World Sources` (2–4 invented sources: author, title, year) — `## New Canon` — `## New Timeline`.
- Every date is written "X AR." No real-world places, faiths, or idioms. No hedging; the encyclopedia is confident even about contested facts (attribute disputes to in-world historians instead).
- **Appendix rule (applied at integration):** each published entry ends with the full current Master Timeline and full current Glossary appended, headed "Appendix: Master Timeline" and "Appendix: Glossary."
- **Audit rule:** after every tenth entry, a 5,000-word consistency audit: every contradicting passage quoted **in full**, verdict on each, and full replacement text for affected passages; affected entries are then rewritten and republished.
- Spellings are locked: Aurelith, Corm, Liral, Veilfall, Veilwork, veinstone, pale fade, Solvenna, Ulzai, Yandu, Zimovets, Qasr-Ilim, Surafta, Skarnholt, Caer Luth, Sovanel, Hearth Delve, Suramala, Vigil, Marrow Sea, Hushed Ocean, Kettledeep Ridges, Pinemarrow, Maw of Grief.
